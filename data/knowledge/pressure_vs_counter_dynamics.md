# Pressure vs Counter Dynamics

Key Concepts:
- Pressure fighters force reactions and create volume-based exchanges.
- Counter strikers rely on timing, accuracy, and punishing mistakes.

Advantages for Pressure Fighters:
- Can overwhelm low-output opponents
- Force counter strikers to move backward
- Create fatigue through pace and pressure

Advantages for Counter Strikers:
- Exploit overextensions
- Land cleaner, more impactful shots
- Control engagements with timing

Critical Factors:
- Footwork efficiency
- Cage-cutting ability
- Defensive responsibility during entries
