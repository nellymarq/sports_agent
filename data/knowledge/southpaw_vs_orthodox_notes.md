# Southpaw vs Orthodox Notes

Key Dynamics:
- Open stance matchups create outside foot battles.
- Rear-hand strikes become more available for both fighters.
- Body kicks and straight punches are high-value weapons.

Southpaw Advantages:
- Left body kick lands clean to the open side
- Rear straight has a direct path
- Angles off to the right reduce opponent's jab effectiveness

Orthodox Advantages:
- Lead hook counters southpaw entries
- Outside low kick disrupts stance
- Double jab can control distance

Important Considerations:
- Foot position dictates exchanges
- Whoever controls the outside angle often wins the striking battle
