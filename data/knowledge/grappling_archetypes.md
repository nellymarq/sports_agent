# Grappling Archetypes in MMA

1. Chain Wrestler
   - Shoots repeatedly
   - Transitions between single/double legs
   - Wins by control and pressure

2. Body Lock Clincher
   - Uses cage control
   - Trips, reaps, and mat returns
   - Wins by wearing opponents down

3. Submission Hunter
   - Aggressive from guard or top
   - Looks for chokes and joint locks
   - Wins by opportunistic finishes

4. Scrambler
   - Creates chaos in transitions
   - Hard to hold down
   - Wins by reversals and momentum shifts
