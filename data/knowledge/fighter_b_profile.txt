Fighter B Profile

Style: High-pace wrestler-boxer.
Strengths:
- Relentless chain wrestling
- Strong top control and ground-and-pound
- Durable with excellent cardio
- Effective jab and overhand right

Weaknesses:
- Can be countered during level changes
- Predictable entries when tired
- Sometimes absorbs unnecessary damage

Tendencies:
- Pushes forward immediately
- Mixes striking and wrestling seamlessly
- Thrives in scrambles and chaotic exchanges
