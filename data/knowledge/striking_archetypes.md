# Striking Archetypes in MMA

1. Pressure Striker
   - Walks forward constantly
   - High volume, high pace
   - Wins by overwhelming opponents

2. Counter Striker
   - Waits for openings
   - Relies on timing and accuracy
   - Wins by punishing mistakes

3. Outside Kicker
   - Controls range with kicks
   - Avoids pocket exchanges
   - Wins by attrition and movement

4. Power Puncher
   - Low volume, high impact
   - Looks for one big moment
   - Wins by knockout potential
