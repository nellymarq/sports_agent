Fighter X Profile

Style: Pressure striker with high output.
Strengths:
- Constant forward pressure
- High cardio and pace
- Strong clinch control
- Durable and recovers quickly

Weaknesses:
- Can be hit clean early
- Sometimes overextends on combinations
- Takedown defense is average

Tendencies:
- Starts fast and builds momentum
- Prefers to fight in the pocket
- Thrives in chaotic exchanges
