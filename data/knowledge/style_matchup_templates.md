# Style Matchup Templates

Pressure Striker vs Counter Striker:
- Pressure fighter must cut cage and avoid overextending.
- Counter striker must maintain distance and punish entries.
- Key factors: footwork, cage control, defensive responsibility.

Wrestler vs Kickboxer:
- Wrestler must close distance safely and chain attempts.
- Kickboxer must maintain range and punish level changes.
- Key factors: stance, timing, takedown defense.

Southpaw vs Orthodox:
- Outside foot position dictates exchanges.
- Rear-hand strikes become high-value weapons.
- Key factors: angles, jab effectiveness, body kicks.
