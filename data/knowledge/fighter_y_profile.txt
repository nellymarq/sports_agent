Fighter Y Profile

Style: Counter striker with strong takedown defense.
Strengths:
- Excellent timing on counters
- Efficient footwork and distance management
- Strong hips and balance against takedowns
- Accurate jab and straight right

Weaknesses:
- Low output at times
- Can be backed up to the fence
- Slow starter in round 1

Tendencies:
- Prefers opponents who overcommit
- Looks for clean counters rather than volume
- Best in rounds 2 and 3
