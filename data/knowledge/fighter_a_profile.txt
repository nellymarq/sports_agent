Fighter A Profile

Style: Southpaw kickboxer with strong body kicks.
Strengths:
- Excellent left body kick and rear straight
- Controls range with teeps and low kicks
- Strong defensive footwork
- Good takedown defense in open space

Weaknesses:
- Struggles when pressured against the fence
- Limited offensive wrestling
- Sometimes slows down in round 3

Tendencies:
- Starts with long-range weapons
- Builds rhythm through feints and kicks
- Prefers opponents who stand at mid-range
