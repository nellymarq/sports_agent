Unified Rules Judging Criteria

1. Effective Striking and Grappling
   - Highest priority
   - Damage, impact, and fight-ending potential

2. Effective Aggression
   - Moving forward with purpose
   - Creating meaningful offense

3. Octagon Control
   - Dictating pace, place, and position

Judges score rounds based on:
- Damage > Volume
- Impact > Activity
- Near finishes > Control time
