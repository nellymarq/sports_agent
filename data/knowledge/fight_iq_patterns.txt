Common Fight IQ Patterns

High Fight IQ Indicators:
- Adjusting strategy mid-fight
- Recognizing opponent tendencies early
- Managing energy efficiently
- Avoiding low-percentage exchanges

Low Fight IQ Indicators:
- Repeating failed entries
- Ignoring corner advice
- Overcommitting when hurt
- Poor round awareness

Impact:
- High fight IQ often determines close fights.
- Low fight IQ leads to avoidable losses even for skilled athletes.
